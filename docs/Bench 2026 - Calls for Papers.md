# Overview
The 18th BenchCouncil International Symposium on Evaluation Science and Engineering (Bench 2026) will be held in Guilin, China, on Nov 6-8, 2026.

Bench is an international symposium organized by the International Open Benchmark Council (BenchCouncil), dedicated to advancing Evaluatology (Evaluation Science and Engineering). The symposium promotes systematic, rigorous, reproducible, and scientifically grounded approaches for evaluation across disciplines, with the goal of establishing evaluation as a unified scientific and engineering practice.

Bench 2026 welcomes original research and engineering contributions in, but not limited to, the following areas:

- Theory and Practice of Evaluation
- Theory and Practice of Testing
- Theory and Practice of Measurement
- Research and Practice in Inference Related to Evaluation, Testing, and Measurement
- Evaluation Science, Metrology, Testing Science, and Inference Science within the Interrogation Scientific Framework
- Development, Applications, and Practices Across Diverse Domains

Researchers from all disciplines and related fields are warmly invited to submit their work.

The conference particularly encourages interdisciplinary research and practical experiences related to evaluation theory, evaluation methodologies, benchmark construction, dataset development, measurement and testing, evaluation standards, performance analysis, and real-world evaluation practices.

Bench has successfully been held for seventeen editions. Proceedings from previous Bench conferences have been published in Springer Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) and indexed by EI Compendex.

Papers accepted within the Bench 2026 review cycle will be included in the Bench 2026 technical program after satisfying the conference presentation requirement.

## Review Policy
Bench 2026 follows a strict double-blind peer-review process. Authors must ensure that submitted manuscripts are fully anonymized. The submitted manuscript must not include:

- Author names
- Affiliations
- Acknowledgments
- Funding information
- Other identifying information

## Submission Requirements

Submitted manuscripts must satisfy the following requirements:

- The manuscript must describe original work that has not been published elsewhere.
- The manuscript must not be under review by another conference, journal, or publication venue at - the time of submission.
- The submitted version must be properly anonymized for double-blind review.
- The manuscript must be submitted as a printable PDF file.
- The manuscript should include page numbers.
- Figures and tables should remain readable when printed in black and white.
- References should provide complete author lists whenever possible and avoid unnecessary use of “et al.”
